# RAG and Embeddings

## Abstract

Vector RAG from the ground up: why an LLM doesn't know your company's documents, how chunking and
embeddings turn text into a map of meaning, and how retrieval puts the right pieces back in the prompt.
Built around a real use case — consultants asking itenium anything — with four questions the naive
pipeline gets wrong, and one that no retriever ever reaches.

## Target Audience

Developers with no prior exposure to RAG, embeddings or vector search. No machine learning background
assumed.

## Key Takeaways

- What an embedding actually is, and why cosine similarity is a proxy for meaning
- Chunking, embedding and retrieval as one pipeline you can reason about end to end
- Where dense retrieval fails, and what hybrid search, reranking and step-back prompting fix
- The questions RAG cannot answer at all — and why that is a graph problem, not a tuning problem

## Session Format

60 minutes
